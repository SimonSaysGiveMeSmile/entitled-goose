The goose lives here now.

An original, code-drawn goose that waddles across your screen, honks at you, judges you silently, and — as a public service — closes your YouTube tabs. You may continue using your computer. For now.

It enforces focus.

YouTube, Instagram, Netflix — the goose marches to the close button, honks twice, and pecks. "closed your youtube. you're welcome."

02

It keeps score.

An Entitlement Meter rises while you ignore it: content → miffed → indignant → wrath. Honks escalate. Footprints appear. Notes are filed.

03

It never blocks you.

A transparent overlay — your cursor passes through everything except the goose itself. Pet it to apologize. It may accept.

04

It remembers.

Quit the app and the next launch opens with: "I noticed you tried to evict me. bold."
