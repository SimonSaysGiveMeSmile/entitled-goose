Notice of Occupancy

Effective immediately · non-negotiable · honk
§1

Focus shall be enforced

YouTube, Instagram, Netflix, TikTok and associated distractions will be closed by beak. The goose walks to the tab's close button, honks twice, and pecks. "closed your youtube. you're welcome." The blocklist is yours to edit.

§2

Moods shall be kept

An Entitlement Meter rises while you ignore the resident: content → miffed → indignant → wrath. Honks escalate. Footprints appear. Complaints are filed in writing, in a speech bubble, with a signature.

§3

The resident shall be aware

It knows the time ("it is 2am. even geese sleep."), watches your battery — delivering warnings at the battery icon itself — counts your absences to the minute, and can remind you of calendar events. All configurable in the control panel.

§4

You shall not be obstructed

The overlay is click-through everywhere except the goose itself, with a failsafe. Pet it (hold ~1s) to apologize. Pick it up and it dangles, unimpressed. Quit, and the next launch opens with: "I noticed you tried to evict me. bold."

Accept the terms. Install the goose.
